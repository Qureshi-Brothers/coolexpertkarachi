Questions

Frequently asked questions

How soon can a technician come?

Call or WhatsApp us with your area and problem. We confirm a visit time on the call, and for emergencies we work 24/7.

How much will the repair cost?

It depends on the fault and the AC model. Our technician checks the unit first and tells you the cost before starting any work.

Do you give a warranty?

Yes. Our work is covered by a service warranty, and we use genuine parts. Ask the technician for the warranty period of your job.

My inverter AC shows an error code. Can you fix it?

Yes. We diagnose the error and repair the inverter PCB card in most cases, which is usually cheaper than replacing the whole board.

How often should an AC be serviced in Karachi?

Karachi's dust and long summers make filters dirty fast. A full service before summer and regular filter cleaning keeps cooling strong and the bill lower. Our maintenance contracts cover this for you.

Do you work for offices and commercial buildings?

Yes. We handle homes, flats, offices, shops and commercial sites, with monthly and annual maintenance contracts available.
